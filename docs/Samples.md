## Ad Inspector

In the MAUI sample on Android or iOS, complete the consent flow and tap **Open Ad Inspector** on a registered test device. Use it to inspect ad requests and mediation adapters. To register your device, see [Enable test devices on Android](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/test-ads#enable_test_devices) or [Enable test devices on iOS](https://developers.google.com/admob/ios/test-ads#enable_test_devices).
